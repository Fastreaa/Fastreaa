## About Me

I'm Emiliano Aguilar, a Computer and Telecommunications Engineering student at Universidad Iberoamericana in Mexico City, interested in building reliable software, data-driven systems, and technology with practical and scientific applications.

My work and projects span several areas of engineering, from backend development and database design to business intelligence, cloud infrastructure, scientific computing, and digital electronics.

I enjoy understanding how systems work, designing solutions to complex problems, and translating technical concepts into useful tools and accessible knowledge.

- **Software Engineering:** Backend development, APIs, database-driven applications, and web technologies.
- **Data Engineering & BI:** SQL, ETL workflows, data modeling, process automation, and analytical reporting.
- **Cloud & Infrastructure:** Cloud services, Linux environments, Docker, integrations, and deployment workflows.
- **Scientific Computing:** Python, ROOT, numerical analysis, and physics data analysis.
- **Computer Engineering:** Digital logic, sequential circuits, hardware experimentation, and embedded systems.
- **Science Communication:** Developing accessible science content and educational initiatives for a community of over 300,000 followers.
- **Digital Entrepreneurship:** Web development, e-commerce, custom digital solutions, and automation.

I'm particularly interested in projects that combine software architecture, data, scientific thinking, and real-world problem-solving.

## Engineering Principles

I aim to approach software development with an emphasis on:

- **Maintainability:** Clear code, modular design, and separation of concerns.
- **Software Architecture:** Appropriate abstractions, SOLID principles, and solutions aligned with project complexity.
- **Data Integrity:** Validation, consistency, traceability, and responsible data handling.
- **Security & Privacy:** Least-privilege access, secure configuration, and protection of sensitive information.
- **Automation:** Reproducible workflows that reduce manual effort and operational errors.
- **Continuous Learning:** Exploring new technologies through practical implementation and experimentation.

## Beyond Software

My interests extend beyond programming into physics, philosophy, scientific research, digital electronics, and critical thinking.

I also enjoy creating educational content, exploring the social impact of technology, and developing independent digital projects.

I believe engineering is not just about making systems work; it is about understanding the problem, making thoughtful design decisions, and building solutions that remain useful over time.
